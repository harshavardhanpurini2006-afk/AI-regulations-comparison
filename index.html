KEY TAKEAWAYS

What stands out?

01

Different policy approaches

The EU has a dedicated AI Act with risk-based obligations. India's approach includes policy guidance and a broader, evolving governance ecosystem.

02

Risk matters

AI systems used in sensitive areas can affect people’s safety and rights. Risk assessment and suitable safeguards are important considerations.

03

Rules can change

AI policy develops over time. Always check the latest official text and implementation dates before making compliance decisions.
